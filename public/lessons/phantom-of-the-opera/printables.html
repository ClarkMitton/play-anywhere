Escape the theatre: answers

Print one copy for yourself. Pages 2, 3 and 4 are the three envelopes. Print them once per table, cut the cards on page 2, and put each table's set in an envelope marked 1, 2 or 3. Nobody writes on the paper. Learners write on the whiteboard table.

1Scene 1 · The dressing room

Order: First (M) → Next (A) → Then (S) → Finally (K). The circled letters spell MASK.

Facts, not orders (no letter): "The Phantom loves music." and "The mirror is very old." Tables that put these in the order have not yet separated an order from a fact. Ask: "Is this telling me to do something?"

2Scene 2 · The map

Route: dressing room → stage (next to) → the lake (under the stage) → the organ (next to the lake). They finish in THE ORGAN. Code word two is ORGAN.

Stretch: Box Five is above the dressing room. Directions might be: "Start in the dressing room. Box Five is above the dressing room. Go there." Accept any correct place words.

3Scene 4 · The last riddle

Correct answer: "ORGAN. MASK." and then knock three times. The room word comes first, the key word second. FIVE is a trap: the note says do not say it.

Look for: order (room word first), the knock, and not saying FIVE. A table that says "MASK, ORGAN" read the order wrong. A table that says "FIVE" did not read the second order.

Setting the room

  • Lights low if you can. Candles are not allowed; use the screens.
  • Read the Phantom's letter in a low voice.
  • Do not translate the cards. Point, mime, say it again slower.

Envelope 3 · Scene 4

The last riddle

P

You are in the cellar.
The door is next to the organ.

  1. First, go to the lake. It is under the stage.
  2. Next, take the boat to the door.
  3. At the door, do not say FIVE.
  4. Say the room word. Then say the key word.
  5. Finally, knock three times.

What do you say? What do you do?

Write it on the table. One of you will type it for the whole room.

Stretch. Write a riddle note for another table. It must have First, Next, Then and Finally, and one place word.
