Before the lesson

  • Print pages 2 and 3 once for each table. Cut page 3 into six cards. Put page 2 and the six cards in one envelope per table.
  • Every learner needs a whiteboard pen. Nothing is written on the paper, so the packs can be used again.

Step 7 · the first video (stops at 1:55)

People in the street say and spell their names, then say how old they are (from about 1:02), then where they are from (from about 1:24). One name, one number and one place per table is a good result.

Step 14 · the morning video

Mark says: I'm always very busy in the morning. I usually get up at 6:10. I always make the bed. I sometimes go for a run. I always take a shower, brush my teeth, make coffee, make breakfast, read the newspaper, check my email, leave for work. From 1:49 learners say the sentences with him.

Step 16 · About me: what a good card has

A name with a capital letter. A country or a town with a capital letter. Three morning things, each with always or sometimes. In round 2 the learner asks all three questions and says their own three sentences.
