Ask this of every idea in the lesson

This is a room, not a slide deck. Three screens show different things at the same time, the tables are for writing, and your phone is the remote. Page 2 is the full toolbox.

LEAD: work through the four phases in order

LaunchA hook that opens a question. Confidence baseline. Share the objectives.
EstablishShort input, then check straight away. Predict before reveal.
ApplyUse it in a new context: table tasks, hotspots, buzzer, harder questions.
DemonstrateLearners show it: a product against criteria, the final confidence check.

The room

  • Big screen: the whole class watches together. Slides, video, live results.
  • Two touch screens: portrait, learners walk up and tap. Both show the same activity: half the class at each.
  • Whiteboard tables: learners stand and write on the table. Two touch screens cannot occupy a whole class; tables can.
  • Your phone (the remote): moves the lesson on, adds a timer over any slide, controls the reveal.
  • Plan where learners are for every step. Aim for two whole-class moves in 45 minutes. Send a runner, not the room, when one answer per table is enough.

2 · The toolbox: everything a screen can do

Show

AnyText slide

A headline, not an essay. Optional title box for context.

AnyPicture (Canva slide)

Make slides in Canva and add them as pictures. Few words, read from across the room.

Big or allYouTube

Trim with start and end times. The lesson moves on when it ends. Big screen + questions on the touch screens = a watching task.

AnyVideo file (loops)

Starts by itself and can loop: welcome screens, music under a walk.

BigAudio (listening)

Play, pause, play again. Never starts by itself, never moves the lesson on.

BigAudio & picture

A sound file over a picture: a voicemail, a scene, a soundscape. Plays only when you press Play.

TouchEmbed (Wordwall, Canva, website)

Anything learners tap goes on the touch screens, never the big screen. Write one line on what it shows.

AnyStandby

Only while you talk. Never on two screens at once.

Ask

TouchConfidence check

Numbers, faces or agree/disagree. Mark one Start and one Final: the big screen compares them.

TouchVote

Opinions and best-answer debates, where two options look right. Live results on the big screen.

Touch 2Single question

Multiple choice or true/false, answered on Touch Screen 2.

Both touchQuestion round

Up to 10 questions on both touch screens. Mix true/false, best answer, odd one out.

TouchWord cloud

Many short answers. Repeated words grow. Everything shows in lower case.

TouchShared board

Longer answers, exactly as typed. Use it to capture the product at the end.

Play

Both touchBuzzer quiz

Two teams, one per touch screen. Fast recall of what has already been taught.

BigWheel spinner

Picks a person, topic or scenario. Spin it again to make a point (same answer every time?).

AllSpot the hazard (hotspots)

Tap to find on a photo. Each touch screen must find every spot; the big screen shows the race and the reveal.

AllSpot the difference

Two matching photos, each screen finds the differences. Photograph it yourself: set up right, then set up wrong.

Touch + bigWhiteboard

Draw, alone or over a picture (floor plans, matching, "draw what is missing"). Send to the big screen; put it there too.

Time & move

AllCountdown

A timed task everyone can see, with a label saying what to do.

AnyRotation timer

Carousel round the tables: rounds, move time, what to do. Do the task, then move and check another table. Hides the slide.

BigBig-screen timer

A timer on the big screen only.

RemoteAdd Timer (from your phone)

Starts a timer over whatever is showing, so a picture stays up during a table task.

How a step ends

You move on

From the remote or Touch Screen 1. The default.

Answers are in

When Touch Screen 2 submits. For questions and checks.

Timed

Moves on by itself. Use rarely: explain before anything starts.

Pick the tool for the jobWord cloud for many short answers. Shared board when wording matters. Vote for opinions. Question round for right answers. Buzzer for fast recall.
Pictures and soundUpload to the app's media storage and use the full address, or add them in the designer. Make people in pictures look like the people in the room.
Nobody is a passengerEvery step gives every screen a job and every learner something to do: watching, moving, writing, tapping, talking, making or judging.

5 · Check it before you hand it over

The lesson

  • One thread. Every clip, slide and task serves the one idea.
  • Objectives climb, with one at analyse or above.
  • LEAD in order. The Launch hook question is answered in the last step.
  • Learners predict or attempt before they are told.
  • Teach before you test: every question follows the step that answers it.
  • Mix the question types: never two rounds of the same shape.
  • No activity twice in a row.
  • Demonstrate is a product against criteria, captured on a shared board.
  • Confidence check at the start and the end.

The room

  • At least two table tasks, and a third of the time at the tables (if tables were agreed).
  • A stretch on every table task.
  • Every screen has a job in every step; standby never on two screens at once.
  • Screen steps grouped into one trip. About two whole-class moves per 45 minutes.
  • A runner, not the room, when one answer per table is enough.
  • Wordwalls and tap games on the touch screens, with the instruction on the big screen.
  • Every video is either full focus (all screens) or a watching task (questions set first, then talked through).
  • The step after a video is the one you want: the lesson moves on when it ends.
  • Inclusive: pictures show a real mix of people; nothing assumes money, a device or a home set-up.

Before it is taught

  • Pictures and sound are in the media storage and open in a browser.
  • Click through the preview: all three screens with the notes for each step.
  • Watch every clip to the end, with start and end times set.
  • Tap every hotspot once and check the ring lands on the right thing.
  • Facts that could change (rules, policies, what a tool does today) checked against the real source.
  • Anything a timer will hide is on the whiteboard first.
  • Printed packs printed and cut, or the lesson works with nothing printed.
  • Props and kit are things the room already has, unless someone has agreed to buy them.

After it is taught

  • What went well, and even better if, written up while it is fresh.
  • Was the length and pace right? What would you add, change or take out?
