# SEND 3 · Canva Code prompt: "Sort the toolkit"

Used in step 32 of [send3.sql](send3.sql), just before the cheat sheets are handed out.
Staff sort the college tools from the three "Tools" slides (13, 19, 25) into the
three groups on slide 27, so the cheat sheet that follows is the answer.

**How to use it:** open Canva, choose Canva Code, paste everything in the box below,
check the result on a tablet, publish it, and send Clark the link. The link then goes
on the tablets (a QR or a shortcut) and into step 32's touch screens as an Embed.

---

```
Build a single-page interactive sorting game for staff training, used on tablets and on large portrait touch screens. British English. No em dashes. No sound. No timer. No login.

TITLE: Sort the toolkit
SUBTITLE: Where does each college tool belong?

THREE GROUPS (large drop zones, side by side on a wide screen, stacked on a tall or small screen):
1. Understand the need
2. Adapt the lesson
3. Learners' own tools

ELEVEN CARDS, shown shuffled in a tray at the top. Each card shows the tool name in large text:
- EBS
- Disability Services
- Student Services
- The learner
- Lesson Weaver
- Go Read
- Class Team posts
- Accessibility Checker
- Digital Companion
- Immersive Reader
- Audio and video feedback in Teams

HOW IT WORKS:
- Tap a card to select it, then tap a group to place it. Dragging should also work. Tapping a placed card sends it back to the tray.
- A "Check my sort" button. Correct cards turn green with a tick. Wrong cards turn orange and shake once, then return to the tray so they can try again. Show a count: "8 of 11 in the right place".
- When all eleven are right, show a short message: "That's your cheat sheet. Hide the pile, no surprises, lower the language, not the thinking." and a "Show why" button.
- "Show why" reveals one line under each card (see ANSWERS).
- A "Start again" button reshuffles.

ANSWERS (group, then the line shown by "Show why"):
- EBS · Understand the need · The support already agreed. Check it before you plan.
- Disability Services · Understand the need · Advice, support agreements and referral. Refer early.
- Student Services · Understand the need · Wellbeing and welfare, not only SEND.
- The learner · Understand the need · Ask them what helps. They are the expert on themselves.
- Lesson Weaver · Adapt the lesson · Plans and scaffolds. Adapt the language, never the level of thinking.
- Go Read · Adapt the lesson · Texts made for your session, at the level you choose.
- Class Team posts · Adapt the lesson · The plan, and any change, posted before learners arrive.
- Accessibility Checker · Adapt the lesson · Run it before you share any document.
- Digital Companion · Learners' own tools · One step at a time, Listen, fonts, a timer, Breathing Space.
- Immersive Reader · Learners' own tools · Reads text aloud in Word, Teams, Forms and Edge.
- Audio and video feedback in Teams · Learners' own tools · Feedback they can listen to, on a few key words.

LOOK AND ACCESSIBILITY:
- Calm, warm design to match the session slides: cream background (#F3EBDD), deep teal headings and group headers (#1F5C57), dark navy text (#1D2B36), one orange accent for buttons (#E07A3F).
- Very large, readable sans-serif text (cards at least 22px, headings at least 36px), generous spacing, high contrast, rounded cards with clear borders.
- Big touch targets (at least 56px high). Works with a finger on a tablet and on a 1080 by 1920 portrait screen.
- No flashing, no auto-playing animation beyond the single shake, nothing on a timer.
- Everything fits on one screen without scrolling on a 1080 by 1920 portrait display and on a landscape tablet.
```
