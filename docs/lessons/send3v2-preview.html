Spin the swap

Applystarts 28:30 · 3 min

Say: The wheel lands on something teachers say. Thirty seconds: rewrite it on your table, then hold it up. The room picks the best.

22

Spot the difference

Applystarts 31:30 · 3 min

Say: Same task sheet, before and after, on the big screen. As a table: list every change, and tag each one H (hide the pile), N (no surprises) or L (lower the language). Then: what is the one thing that did not change?

Watch for: Learners: at the tables, no move. The touch screens show each sheet close up for a runner who wants a better look. Answers, with the strategy each uses: the plan, the time and what finished looks like at the top (N); Step 1 of 4, one step at a time (H); tick boxes (H); key words taught first (L); the matrix on the sheet, not copied from the board (L); Listen to this sheet (L); write it or record it (L); a clear layout (L). Some fit two: accept a good argument. Unchanged: the thinking (still rate, control, justify, summarise). Ask one table for its tags, then another to challenge one. Technique: analyse a worked example before building your own.
